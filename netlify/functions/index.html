Frequently Asked Questions ❓

Quick answers to your most pressing wave questions.

What is the best way to get 360 waves?

Consistency is key! Regular brushing with a quality brush (like Ask Guru's), moisturizing your hair, and wearing a durag are fundamental. The "Wave Journey Planner AI" can give you a personalized routine.

How often should I brush my waves?

Aim for at least 30 minutes to an hour a day, spread throughout the day. Consistency over duration is more important. Brush in the direction of your waves to train your hair.

How do I prevent or fix forks?

Forks often indicate inconsistent brushing patterns or insufficient compression. Use a mirror to identify the direction of your hair at the fork and brush from that point outwards. Our "Brush Angle Guide AI" can provide precise angles to help!

What products should I use for waves?

A good moisturizer or wave pomade is essential for laid hair and shine. Combine this with a moisturizing shampoo (like our upcoming Whip Shampoo!) and a quality conditioner. The right brush firmness (hard, medium, soft) for your hair type is also critical.
